OUR METHODOLOGY

Your Journey to Becoming a Certified Pilot

From enrollment to job placement, we offer a comprehensive training timeline tailored for success.

STEP 01

Registration & Onboarding

Select your desired specialty course and register online to reserve your training drone slot.

STEP 02

Theory & Regulations

Learn weather dynamics, air traffic regulations, safety checks, and basic aerodynamics.

STEP 03

Flight Simulator Training

Build muscle memory in our simulated environment to safely master wind and controls.

STEP 04

Practical Flying Labs

Fly real multirotor & fixed-wing training drones under close supervision on open air fields.

STEP 05

DGCA Assessment

Prove your piloting mastery in front of certified external examiners to secure certification.

STEP 06

Career Placement

Receive CV drafting assistance and sit for interviews with our spatial and industrial drone partners.
