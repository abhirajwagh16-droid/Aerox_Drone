OUR ORIGIN

Who We Are

Aero Drone Academy was founded in 2021 by aerospace professionals with a unified purpose: to make professional drone training accessible, safe, and aligned with industrial standards. We cultivate professional UAV operators equipped with technical knowledge of avionics, rules, and flight telemetry.

Today, we operate a 10-acre open flight school equipped with high-performance simulators, drone building workshops, and specialized training payloads (multi-spectral mapping, agricultural nozzles, thermal sensors).

Our Mission

To build a safe, compliant, and highly skilled ecosystem of drone operators capable of driving innovation across agricultural, civil mapping, defensive, and cinematographic sectors.

Our Vision

To stand as the leading global center of excellence in unmanned aviation education, training students from school-age STEM workshops to elite commercial UAV command operations.

METHODOLOGY

Our Core Pillars of Education

Flight Safety First

We build safety checks and pre-flight calibrations into the muscle memory of every pilot trainee.

Hands-on Assembly

Students build, tune, calibrate and repair real UAV frames to understand physical aerodynamics.

Advanced Simulators

Over 10 hours of simulation log prior to real field throttle control to prevent structural failures.
