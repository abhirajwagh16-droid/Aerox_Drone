STEM EDUCATION

Junior Drone Pilot Program (Grades 5th-10th)

Spark young minds with aerodynamics! We teach the science of flight, component engineering, simulator maneuvers, and safety protocols in a fun, interactive curriculum.

1. The Science of Flight

Learn how planes and drones stay in the air using physics:

  • Lift: Created by fast spinning propellers pushing air downwards.
  • Weight (Gravity): The downward pull of Earth that lift must overcome.
  • Thrust: The forward force propelling the drone through space.
  • Drag: Air resistance slowing the drone down.

2. Flight Controls Explained

Drones are steered using four fundamental joystick controls:

  • Throttle: Controls altitude (moving the drone UP or DOWN).
  • Yaw: Controls direction (spinning the drone LEFT or RIGHT).
  • Pitch: Controls tilt (tilting the drone FORWARD or BACKWARD).
  • Roll: Controls lean (sliding the drone LEFT or RIGHT side-to-side).

3. Pre-Flight Safety Checklist

Every professional pilot runs checks before powering on the motors:

  • Inspect the drone frame for structural cracks.
  • Verify propellers are tightened securely and oriented correctly.
  • Check battery levels of both the drone and radio controller.
  • Inspect flight zone to ensure it's clear of crowds, buildings, and tall power lines.
